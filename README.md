# WA Race Map 2026–27

Interactive map of the 37 Western Australian trail, road and ultra races on the
Tribe&Trail WA Race Calendar (Oct 2026 – Sep 2027).

Live page: https://mattsnelson.github.io/wa-race-map/

- Filter by season (the three calendar posters), format, or series
  (Perth Trail Series, Ultra Series, Rapid Ascent).
- Drawn basemap or Esri satellite imagery.
- Link shortcuts: `#pts`, `#ultraseries`, `#rapid` open on a series;
  `#sw` / `#metro` open zoomed; `#sat` starts on satellite.

Everything is in `index.html` — race data is the `EVENTS` array near the top of
the script, so adding or correcting a race is a one-line edit.

Coastline from Natural Earth (public domain). Satellite tiles are Esri World
Imagery (Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community).
